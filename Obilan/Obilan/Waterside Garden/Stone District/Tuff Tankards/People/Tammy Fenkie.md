**Name:** Tammy Fenkie

**Race:** Tabaxi

	She was once innocent and pure but lately survival has called for something more profitable, after her parents were kidnapped and no one was able to pay the ransom she's took up the job of hero though learning alone seems harder than she thought. She wears a sort of shoddy looking hood trying to be inconspicuous.
