**Name:** A Lizard Named Slickback

**Race:** Lizard folk

**General information:**
	A blackjack dealer at [[Tuff Tankards]] that absolutely refuses to be called anything else, he is a sly looking very well dressed Lizard folk wearing a very nice fancy purple suit. He had big dreams as a kid to grow very wealthy building businesses but so far he's really only learned to swindle and prey on desperation to gain his enormous wealth. 

**Extra Information:**
	[[Javier Lammers]] knows about his swindles and scams but Jack pays handsomely to operate out of [[Tuff Tankards]].
