**Name:** Tuff ***(Ward Storrie)***

**Race:** Stone Golem ***(Orc)*** 

**General information:**
	A stone golem sitting in the tavern unable to speak arm wrestling anyone who challenges him. They say he's never lost but no one really knows his origin as he just came in one day and [[Javier Lammers]] doesn't kick him out because he bring in lots of people. His only notible features are two shiny green gems for eyes. 

**Extra Information:**
	He is cursed by a green stone to be stone completely unrecognizable and unable to speak, his wife Glorianna Storrie a cook in the royal castle doesn't recognize so he's decided to rest at Tuff Tankards as his life has been completely revoked from him, when all he was trying to do is provide for his wife.
